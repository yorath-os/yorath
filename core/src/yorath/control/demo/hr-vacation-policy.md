# Vacation Policy (HR demo set)

**Data class:** Internal. **Owner:** HR department. **Version:** 2.3 of 2026-09-01.

This document is part of the Yorath HR demo. All figures and rules are fictional and exist only to test Document Assistant.

## 1. Annual paid vacation

1.1. Annual paid vacation is **28 calendar days**. Employees of the Field Sales department get **3 extra calendar days** because their work involves travel.

1.2. Vacation may be split into parts. At least one part must be **at least 14 calendar days** long. Other parts may be of any length, but no shorter than 1 day.

1.3. A vacation request is submitted through the HR portal **at least 14 calendar days** before the vacation starts. For a vacation of up to 3 days the notice period is 5 calendar days.

1.4. The request is approved by the direct manager. If the manager does not respond within 3 business days, the request is approved automatically.

1.5. Unused days carry over to the next year. An employee can accumulate **no more than 56 days**; days above that limit expire on December 31.

## 2. Unpaid leave

2.1. Granted by agreement with the manager and the department's HR Business Partner. The maximum is **30 calendar days per year**.

2.2. Unpaid leave does not reduce the service period used to calculate annual vacation if its total length during the year does not exceed 14 days.

## 3. Sick leave and family care

3.1. When sick, an employee notifies their manager on the first day of absence by any means; the sick note is uploaded to the HR portal within 3 business days after returning.

3.2. The first **3 days** of sickness are paid by the company at 100% of salary; after that, social insurance rules apply.

## 4. Who can see vacation information

| Role | Own vacation | Direct reports' vacations | Assigned departments' vacations | All vacations |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Employee | yes | no | no | no |
| Manager | yes | yes | no | no |
| HRBP | yes | yes | yes, assigned departments only | no |
| HR Administrator | yes | yes | yes | yes |

4.1. An HR Business Partner is assigned to one or more departments by order of the HR director. The list of assignments is kept in the HR system.

4.2. Vacation balances are personal data with the data class **Confidential**. Sending them to external services, including external LLMs, is prohibited.

## 5. Contacts

- Balance calculations: HR Administrator, hr-admin@example.com.
- Approvals: direct manager, then the department's HRBP.
- Policy changes are approved by the HR director.
